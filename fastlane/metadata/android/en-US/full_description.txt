An unofficial companion for teams playing FIRST LEGO League BIOGLOW.

FEATURES
• Score Founders and Future Edition missions.
• Review mission details, rules and scoring notes.
• Use the 2:30 match timer and practice guide.
• Open official model-building instruction resources.
• Pick an edition and colour theme during first-run setup.

FLL Helper is an independent community project and is not endorsed by FIRST or the LEGO Group. Game materials and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
